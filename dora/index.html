Why a US or UK vendor gets this questionnaire

DORA applies to EU financial entities, not to you. But Article 28 says they remain fully responsible for compliance when they outsource, and that responsibility travels down the contract.

Article 28

Your client must assess you before signing, keep a register of information on every ICT contract, and have documented, tested exit strategies for services that support critical or important functions.

Article 29

Your client must weigh concentration risk, including when you rely on subcontractors established in a third country.

Article 30

Every ICT contract must carry minimum clauses: service description, data locations, data return, incident assistance, audit and termination rights. More apply when you support a critical or important function.

Frequently asked questions

Does DORA apply to a US SaaS company?

Not directly, unless you are designated as a critical ICT third-party provider. DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) applies to EU financial entities. Articles 28 to 30 make them manage the risk of their ICT vendors and put specific clauses in every contract, so the requirements reach you through the contract and the due-diligence questionnaire.

What does a DORA vendor questionnaire usually ask?

Where your data and processing are located, including subcontractors; your subcontractor list; when you last tested your continuity plan and with what result; your measured recovery time and data loss; whether you can return all of a client's data at the end of the contract; your incident procedure; and audit rights.

What is a DORA exit strategy?

For ICT services that support critical or important functions, Article 28(8) requires the financial entity to have documented exit strategies, and Article 30(3) requires a mandatory, adequate transition period in the contract. In practice your client will ask whether you can hand over its data in a usable format and support a migration.

Are we a critical ICT third-party provider?

Almost certainly not if you are a typical SaaS vendor. The European Supervisory Authorities published their first list of 19 designated providers on November 18, 2025, mostly large cloud, data center, network and financial-technology providers. Your own cloud provider may be on it.

How long does the evidence pack take?

Three weeks: one week of inventory, one week of tests (restore, data export), one week to write the evidence file and, if you take the larger pack, the questionnaire answers.

Is the evidence pack a certification?

No. It is a set of dated tests and documents you can attach to your answers. Reprisa is not a law firm or an auditor, and the pack is not a certification or a legal opinion.
